# Review guide

Who this is for: the human expert who decides whether a staged skill may be
published. The pipeline is built so that this decision is yours, not the
model's.

## Lifecycle

```
draft → security-cleared → source-verified → expert-reviewed → published
                                              (deprecated from anywhere)
```

- `validate` advances draft → security-cleared → source-verified
  automatically when the deterministic checks pass. It never goes further.
- `mark-reviewed` records your review (refuses while high-impact claims are
  unreviewed, unless `--force`).
- `publish` runs the final deterministic gate and copies the skill to a
  live skills root. Regeneration resets any state to `draft`.

## What to read

`review/REVIEW.md`, generated by `validate`, ranks what needs eyes:

1. **High-impact claims** — clinical recommendations, cross-source
   syntheses, editorial inferences, contested claims. These are where a
   fluent distortion does the most damage.
2. **Low-confidence claims** (< 0.7) and modality flags (causal inflation,
   lost qualifications) — check the paraphrase against the quoted evidence;
   `source-to-skill trace CLM-... --skill <dir>` shows both.
3. **Contradictions** — verify the skill preserves genuine disagreements.
   An empty contradictions section on a rich source is itself suspicious.
4. **Security findings** — source-scan findings explain what was in the
   documents; output-scan findings block publication until resolved.

## Acting on claims

```bash
source-to-skill approve CLM-000481 --skill <dir> --reviewer you@org
source-to-skill reject  CLM-000482 --skill <dir> --reviewer you@org --reason "Overstates causality"
source-to-skill annotate CLM-000483 --skill <dir> --reviewer you@org --note "Holds only for group formats"
source-to-skill mark-reviewed <dir> --reviewer you@org
source-to-skill publish <dir> --to ~/.claude/skills
```

## Decision persistence

Decisions are stored append-only in `review/decisions.jsonl`, keyed to the
content hash of the claim's supporting evidence at decision time. On
regeneration or fold-in:

- unchanged passage → your decision re-applies automatically;
- materially changed passage → the decision is **invalidated** and the
  claim returns to `unreviewed` (you'll see it in the next REVIEW.md);
- claim removed → the decision is reported as orphaned.

You never re-review what didn't change, and nothing changed slips through
carrying an old approval.

## What the gate enforces without you

Even after `mark-reviewed`, `publish` refuses while any of these hold:
unresolved high/critical output findings; medium findings without the
`--findings-reviewed` attestation; ledger validation failures; unresolvable
citation markers. These are deterministic and cannot be overridden from the
CLI.
